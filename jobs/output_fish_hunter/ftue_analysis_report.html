Interpretation (All users)

  • Onboarding is fast: half of new users fire their first bullet within 31 s of account creation (90% within 3.9 min).
  • First kill comes almost immediately: 78% of users kill at least one fish in their first session, with a median of 5 s after the first bet; 65% of those killers get it within 10 seconds. Early kill feedback is effectively guaranteed by game design.
  • High-value kills (fish value ≥ 500) are a rare, late event: only 8.5% of users reach one, at a median of 5.2 min into play — long after the median player has already stopped (94 s of play).
  • The first session is short for most: median first-session play time is 94 s, and 54% of users stop within 2 minutes of their first bet; the p90 session runs 17.8 min.
  • Deposit conversion: 28.2% of users make at least one deposit in the observed window (median 8.2 min after account creation), 13.5% make a second, and 77.7% reach a first withdrawal.
  • High-value kills coincide with much longer sessions: users who hit one play a median of 15.6 min vs 76 s for everyone else. This is correlation, not causation — longer play also gives more chances to hit a big fish.
  • Caveat: only the first bet session is observed, scanned up to 12 h after first play, so “stop play” means the end of the first session, not churn; users may return later.
